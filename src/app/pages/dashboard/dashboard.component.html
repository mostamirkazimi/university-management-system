Balkh University Information

Balkh University is one of the most important and largest institutions of higher education in Afghanistan. It is located in the city of Mazar-i-Sharif and plays a key role in providing academic opportunities for students, especially in the northern region of the country. The university was established in the late 1980s (around 1987) and has since grown into a respected academic center. It offers a wide range of faculties, including Medicine, Engineering, Computer Science, Economics, Law and Political Science, Literature, Journalism, Agriculture, Veterinary Science, and Education. This diversity allows thousands of students from different provinces to pursue their higher education at Balkh University. Balkh University operates under the Ministry of Higher Education and is known for training skilled professionals in various fields. It is particularly recognized for its strong programs in Medicine, Engineering, and Information Technology (IT). Overall, Balkh University is considered a valuable choice for students who want to continue their higher education within Afghanistan, thanks to its academic environment, experienced lecturers, and variety of study programs.
